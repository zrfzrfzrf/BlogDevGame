## Reflection
This is our last week. In just a few days, we completed a large number of tests and developments, and we are very proud of our team members.

Ultimately, the game still has some imperfections - for instance, the beam attack does not inflict damage on the player, and there are issues with the animation effects of some controls. However, overall, our completion rate is very good(compared to other group that I played), and this is largely attributed to the decision we made in the 7th and 8th weeks to streamline the levels.
